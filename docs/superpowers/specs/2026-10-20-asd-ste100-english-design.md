# ASD-STE100-inspired English (principal rule)

## Intent

Make Simplified Technical English the principal style for English that the agent writes in this repo.

The style follows ASD-STE100 ideas. It is not a claim of STE certification. It does not require the licensed STE dictionary.

## Done when

- `.cursor/rules/41-asd-ste100-english.mdc` exists with `alwaysApply: true`.
- The rule tells the agent to use short, clear English and to keep technical terms.
- `AGENTS.md` points to that rule as the principal English style.
- Spanish product copy stays under `40-neutral-spanish-copy.mdc`.

## Out of scope

- A bulk rewrite of `src/dictionaries/en.json`.
- A change to Spanish copy.
- Purchase or enforcement of the official ASD-STE100 word list.
