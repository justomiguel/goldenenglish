# Mobile admin bar

**Intent:** On a narrow screen, the admin bar shows the menu, a small logo, and the alerts icon. The view-as control and the messages icon stay on a wide screen.
**Done when:** Below the `md` breakpoint, admin chrome hides the workspace switch, the messages link, the site link, and the search trigger. A small logo sits in that bar. Teacher, assistant, and portal bars also hide the workspace switch on a narrow screen. Wide screens keep the current controls. Tests lock the mobile hide classes and the logo.
**Out of scope:** View-as permissions, the read-only banner, and the desktop popover.
