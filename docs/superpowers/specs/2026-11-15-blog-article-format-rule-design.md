# Blog article format rule

## Intent

Future blog notes follow one writing rule. The answer is underlined. The facts are bold. Each photo stays whole and centered.

## Done when

`.cursor/rules/54-blog-article-format.mdc` is `alwaysApply: true` and states that pattern.

## Out of scope

A change to the live articles. A change to the photo components. Those already use `object-contain`.
